## Design Context

### Users
Trosky serves hotel revenue analysts and hotel stakeholders. Analysts need a fast commercial cockpit for rates, competitors, occupancy, pace, events, promotions, and inquiries across a portfolio. Clients need a focused, trustworthy read-only view for their own hotel and inquiry workflow.

### Brand Personality
Confident, precise, premium. The interface should feel like revenue intelligence with authority: sharp enough for analysts, calm enough for clients, and visually owned by the Trosky red ribbon logo.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a premium red-and-graphite system. Primary red is `#A60101`; darker red `#780000` anchors destructive states and dark-mode primary surfaces; foreground ink is `#424040`. Dark mode should feel warm black and red, not blue-black. Charts use red for Trosky / Our Hotel, teal-emerald for AI recommendations, warm graphite for comp average, and violet for occupancy.

### Design Principles
- Make Trosky branding visible in primary surfaces, not only as tiny navigation text.
- Treat red as confident brand authority, not constant alarm.
- Preserve chart readability with purposeful contrast: red, teal, graphite, violet, amber.
- Add motion for orientation and polish, while respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep operational screens dense, scannable, and calm; save visual drama for landing and key dashboard moments.
